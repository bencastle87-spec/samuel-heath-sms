Terms of Service (SMS Maintenance Paging)

1. Program Description: Samuel-Heath Maint Alerts sends automated internal operational text messages from manufacturing line HMIs/PLCs to opted-in maintenance technicians and supervisors when equipment requires assistance or repair.

2. Opt-In & Cancellation: Employees voluntarily opt in by texting START to our dedicated facility alert number shown on the Maintenance Shop Dispatch Board and Shift Onboarding Packet. You can cancel the SMS service at any time by texting STOP. After sending STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts will be sent unless you text START again.

3. Help & Support: If you experience issues with the messaging program, reply with the keyword HELP or contact your Maintenance Supervisor.

4. Carrier Liability & Rates: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you.

SMS Privacy Policy

1. Data Collected: For the Samuel-Heath Maint Alerts program, we collect only your mobile phone number and opt-in status when you text START to enroll.

2. Strict No-Sharing / No-Sale Guarantee: No mobile information, telephone numbers, or SMS opt-in data and consent will be shared with, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes under any circumstances. Data is shared solely with our SMS gateway provider (Twilio) strictly as necessary to transmit operational maintenance alerts.
